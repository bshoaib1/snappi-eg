💡 Creator FAQs

Before you apply.

Need help? Email .

Do I need a large following?+

No. Snappi focuses on content quality, communication, category fit, and the ability to create authentic brand content.

Why do I need a Google account?+

The initial application uses Google Forms, and Google requires sign-in when the form includes a profile-photo upload.

Will my profile photo appear publicly?+

Not automatically. Snappi reviews it privately and needs separate permission before publishing creator profile information.

Does approval guarantee paid work?+

No. Approval places you in the creator network. Campaign opportunities depend on brand needs, fit, timing, and availability.
