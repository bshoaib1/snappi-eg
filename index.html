Why Snappi?+

We handle the path from brief to execution so brands can focus on the campaign while creators work from clear direction.

How can Snappi help my brand grow?+

Snappi helps brands produce authentic creator content designed to improve attention, trust, and campaign consistency.

How does the process work?+

Choose a package, share your brief, review creator options, approve the direction, and receive the agreed deliverables.

Can I choose the creators?+

Yes. Snappi presents options based on your brand and campaign, and you approve the final creator selection.

How long does content take?+

Most launch packages take approximately 15–20 business days after the brief and project details are finalized.

Do I get revisions?+

Launch packages include up to two revision rounds within the agreed campaign scope.
