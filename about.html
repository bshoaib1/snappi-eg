🎯 Our purpose

Make creator collaboration clearer.

Snappi aims to reduce unnecessary friction between an idea, the right creator voice, and content that is ready to use.

Mission

Help brands and creators work together with clarity.

We are creating a straightforward path for briefs, creator matching, communication, review, and delivery.

Vision

Make authentic creator content easier to build and trust.

We want good creative partnerships to feel organized, respectful, and useful for everyone involved.
