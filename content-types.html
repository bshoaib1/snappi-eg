🧩 Format library

Ten useful ways to tell the story.

A campaign can use one format or combine several formats. Final recommendations depend on the product, audience, objective, and channels.

01 · 🧴

Product demonstration

Show the product in use while making its key functions and benefits easy to understand.

Useful for: education and consideration
02 · 💬

Product review

Share a natural creator perspective about the product experience, features, and everyday value.

Useful for: trust and consideration
03 · 🎁

Unboxing

Capture the package opening, what is included, and the creator’s genuine first impression.

Useful for: launches and product discovery
04 · 🪜

Tutorial

Guide viewers through a clear sequence that explains how to use the product or service.

Useful for: education and reducing uncertainty
05 · ✨

Problem and solution

Open with a relatable frustration, then position the product as a practical way forward.

Useful for: attention and conversion
06 · ⭐

Testimonial

Tell a personal experience that communicates how the product or service helped the creator.

Useful for: credibility and reassurance
07 · 🌤️

Lifestyle integration

Place the product naturally inside a routine, setting, or moment that fits the audience.

Useful for: awareness and brand affinity
08 · 🎙️

Voiceover video

Combine product-focused footage with a clear voiceover that explains the story or benefits.

Useful for: polished storytelling and demos
09 · 📝

List or tips

Package several benefits, ideas, or recommendations into a fast and easy-to-follow structure.

Useful for: retention and saveable content
10 · 🧪

Ad variations

Create alternate hooks, messages, or calls to action around one core creative direction.

Useful for: testing and paid campaigns

🧭 How to choose

Start with the campaign objective.

The strongest format is the one that helps the viewer take the next intended step.

Make people notice

Try an unboxing, lifestyle integration, or a strong problem-and-solution hook.

Explain the product

Try a product demonstration, tutorial, voiceover, or tips format.

Build confidence

Try a product review, testimonial, or detailed demonstration.

Test paid creative

Build several hook, message, and call-to-action variations around one offer.

💬 Format FAQs

A few useful distinctions.

Need help choosing?

Do I need to choose the format myself?+

No. Share the product, objective, audience, channels, and examples you like. Snappi can recommend a practical format mix for discussion.

Can one campaign include several formats?+

Yes. The agreed package can combine formats when the final number of videos, creators, production requirements, and scope allow it.

Does a content type guarantee campaign results?+

No specific performance result is guaranteed. The format helps align the creative approach, while performance also depends on the offer, audience, media strategy, product, and execution.

Are paid advertising rights automatically included?+

Usage rights are confirmed separately in the proposal or contract, including platforms, duration, and any creator permissions.
