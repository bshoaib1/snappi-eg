💬 Brand FAQs

Before we begin.

Need something specific?

Can I approve the creators?+

Yes. Snappi presents the creator direction based on your campaign requirements, and the final selection is confirmed before production.

How long does a campaign take?+

Timing depends on the selected package, creator availability, product delivery, and approval speed. The expected schedule is confirmed before production starts.

Are revisions included?+

The launch packages currently include up to two revision rounds within the agreed brief and campaign scope.

Can I use the videos in paid ads?+

Paid advertising rights can be included when the platforms, duration, and any creator permissions are agreed in writing.

Does a package include creator posting?+

Creator posting is separate from content production unless it is specifically included in the written proposal or contract.
